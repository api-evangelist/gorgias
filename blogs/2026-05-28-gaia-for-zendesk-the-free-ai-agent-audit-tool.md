Gaia for Zendesk is a free Chrome extension from Gorgias that audits a Zendesk ticket history to find gaps in AI Agent configuration, producing guidances, instructions, and voice-of-customer insights for review before implementation. It installs in about five minutes, completes analysis in under a minute, and stores credentials locally rather than on Gorgias servers.
